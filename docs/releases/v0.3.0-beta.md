The third beta of RFA-Mod: mods and an editor for the Steam version of Red Faction: Armageddon. Nothing in your game folder is ever changed; the mods work in the game's memory after it starts.

**Download:** `RFA-Mod-v0.3.0-beta.zip` below.

## What's new since v0.2.1-beta

**A first look at Red Faction: Guerrilla's open world in Armageddon (work in progress).** If you own Red Faction: Guerrilla Steam Edition, the new `editor\GuerrillaWorld.exe` builds its map as a new world for Armageddon, called `rfg`, from your own copy. No Guerrilla files are in this download: the tool reads your game and writes the world on your PC. What you get:
- **The whole map at its true size:** all 102 of Guerrilla's zones, its ground with its own ground and cliff textures, and about 8,800 of its rocks. Armageddon's zones are half the size of Guerrilla's; the `guerrilla_world` mod gives this world Guerrilla's zone size, so the map fits as Guerrilla laid it out.
- **Guerrilla's sky:** its sky colours, clouds and far mountains, a day that passes (half an hour by default), and the sun's and the sky's light on the ground. Armageddon lights its levels with lamps and never draws a sky for a world, so the mod draws these itself.
- **A longer view:** the zones around you stay loaded about 500 m out (Armageddon keeps only the zones next to you), with low-detail ground for the far ones.

**Not there yet:** buildings, roads, people, vehicles and plants; the rocks have no collision (you walk through them); the map ends about a kilometre away, beyond what is loaded around you; some flat rock plates poke out of steep slopes as thin dark shards. This is the ground and the sky, to walk and fly around in. More of Guerrilla comes next.

**Guerrilla's HUD, a first piece:** in the Guerrilla world, Guerrilla's own health display takes over from Armageddon's: its thin red health bar at the top middle of the screen, which shows when you're hurt (and for a few seconds when you arrive), shrinks with your health, flashes when it runs low and fades away once you're healed, and the dark-red blood that creeps in from the screen's corners as your health drops. Armageddon's health ring, its red screen edges and its compass bar are hidden there (the compass's place is where Guerrilla's bar goes; Guerrilla's minimap comes later). For everything else (crosshair, ammo, weapon and Nano Forge selectors), and in every other world, you have Armageddon's HUD as usual. Like the world, it's made from your own copy of Guerrilla, by `editor\GuerrillaHud.exe`; `editor\GuerrillaHud.exe --remove` takes it away again. Still to come: Guerrilla's reticles and ammo, weapon wheel, minimap and messages.

**A new README**, shorter and about using the mod, with step-by-step guides; the technical details moved to `docs\how-it-works.md` and the editor's guide to `docs\editor-guide.md` (both in the zip).

**Mods**
- `guerrilla_hud`, see `[GuerrillaHud]` in `rfa_mods.ini`: shows Guerrilla's HUD in the worlds named by `Worlds=` (the Guerrilla world, `rfg`), once `editor\GuerrillaHud.exe` has made it.
- `guerrilla_world`, see `[GuerrillaWorld]` in `rfa_mods.ini`. One thing it does affects every world: Armageddon's physics only reach 2,295 m from the middle of a world, too little for Guerrilla's map, so the mod widens that to 4,599 m when the game starts, campaign included. `ZoneSize=0` turns that (and the larger zones) off if you don't use the Guerrilla world.

## Trying the Guerrilla world

1. You need **Red Faction: Guerrilla Steam Edition** installed (the original, `Red Faction Guerrilla` in your Steam library; Re-MARS-tered's files aren't read yet), and about 750 MB free where RFA-Mod is.
2. In `rfa_mods.ini`, set `GuerrillaDir=` under `[Launcher]` (just below `GameDir=`) to Guerrilla's folder, for example `C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Red Faction Guerrilla`. Both tools read it. (Or give the folder to each tool as its argument.)
3. Run `editor\GuerrillaWorld.exe`. It takes about a minute.
4. Run `editor\GuerrillaHud.exe` for Guerrilla's HUD (a few seconds). `editor\GuerrillaHud.exe --remove` takes it away again; delete its files only that way, since one of them now also holds the pictures of Armageddon's own HUD.
5. Start the game with `RFAModLauncher.exe`, open the developer console with the ` key (from the main menu is fine) and type `level rfg_12_11`.

Run the tools again after updating RFA-Mod. Removing `overrides\build\pc\cache\rfg_*`, `zonescript_rfg.vpp_pc`, `rfg.gtodx` and `rfg_default.dtodx` takes the world away again.

## You need

- The Steam version of the game on the **publicbeta** branch: in Steam, right-click the game, then Properties → Game Versions & Betas → **publicbeta**. On other builds the game runs normally and the mods leave it alone.
- Windows 10 or 11.
- For the editor and its tools: the 64-bit (x64) [.NET 10 Desktop Runtime](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/10.0).

## Installing

Unzip into a folder of your own and set `GameDir=` in `rfa_mods.ini` to your game folder (or start the editor once and pick the folder there). Or unzip it straight into the game folder. Then run `RFAModLauncher.exe` with Steam running. The README has the details.

**Updating from v0.2.x:** the zip holds a fresh `rfa_mods.ini`, laid out more tidily, with new sections (`[GuerrillaWorld]`, `[GuerrillaHud]`) and a new `GuerrillaDir=` under `[Launcher]`. If you keep your own, the new mods use their defaults; add `GuerrillaDir=` under `[Launcher]` (or give the tools the folder) to build the world.

## Please note

- This is a beta, and the Guerrilla world a first look: expect rough edges, and please report problems as an issue here. `logs\rfa_mods.log`, a crash's `logs\crash-*.dmp` and `version.txt` help.
- For single-player only. Don't use the mods in online multiplayer.
