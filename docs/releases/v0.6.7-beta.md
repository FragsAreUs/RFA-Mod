A beta of RFA-Mod: mods and an editor for the Steam version of Red Faction: Armageddon. Nothing in your game folder is ever changed; the mods work in the game's memory after it starts.

**Download:** `RFA-Mod-v0.6.7-beta.zip` below.

## What's new since v0.6.6-beta

**Transporter, Guerrilla's timed delivery.**
- Each of its 18 routes has its car parked at the start. Get in to start the run, and drive it to the safehouse before the clock runs out.
- The corner panel shows the time and the distance, the drop-off point has Guerrilla's marker on the ground, and its icon shows on the minimap and the world map.
- Stop inside the marker to win. Guerrilla's results screen counts up the pro, target, best and your time, and pays your salvage.
- You fail if time runs out, you're out of the car for 10 seconds, the car is wrecked, or you die.
- **RETRY** puts you back at the start with a fresh car, as in Guerrilla. **EXIT** after a death wakes you at the nearest safehouse.

**Free activities.**
- **Killing spree:** kill 3 enemies within 6 seconds and it starts; each kill keeps it going. When it ends, the district's morale rises by your kills.
- **Mayhem:** wreck the EDF's property fast enough and the damage counts up in millions; the more you wreck, the more morale you earn.
- **Car bomb:** every third radio tag you find shows a M.O.A.B. Drive a car onto it to arm it, then blow the car up.
- Guerrilla's diversions panel shows each one as it runs.

**People and damage, as in Guerrilla.**
- You can hurt and kill civilians and guerrillas. Killing one costs the district's morale.
- The EDF's tanks, rockets and cars can hurt their own soldiers.

**Also:**
- Mason sprints as long as you hold the button in the Guerrilla world. Armageddon's levels keep their limit.
- Fixed: a blurry, half-dark minimap in parts of Parker. The game had room for too few interface pictures, and some zones' minimap pictures were dropped.
- Fixed: "Failed pathfind" errors on screen at Parker.
- Guerrilla's text sizes on its results, demolitions master, district-liberated and world map screens.
- In the Guerrilla world, Armageddon's Steam achievements and profile statistics no longer count, so none unlock there by mistake. They work as before in Armageddon's own levels.
- The nine Guerrilla mods are now one file, `mods\rfa_guerrilla.dll`. Each part keeps its own setting under `[Mods]` and its own settings section.

Everything from v0.6.6-beta is here too: sounds that fade with distance, Guerrilla's ground for the cars, sun shafts, wheel dust, placed effects, gusts, barrels and propane tanks.

**Known problems:**
- **Five of the Transporter routes** have extras in Guerrilla (EDF or Marauder chasers, alert triggers, roadblocks). They come with later steps, and run as plain deliveries until then.
- **Picking up a propane tank for the first time** can put it in your weapon select, with a large shape stuck to Mason's hands. Change weapons, then pick the tank up again.
- **Cars stop dead at walls**, and at many signs and poles, instead of breaking through.
- **Steering may still be rough at speed.**
- **Guerrilla's walkers walk on the spot** when you drive one.
- **Mason can't take cover** against walls yet.
- **Dying during a demolitions master challenge** ends it, rather than offering a retry as Guerrilla does.
- The activities, the EDF's reinforcements and the vehicle drop-off are new, and haven't had much testing in game yet.
- **Mission briefing terminals** do nothing yet: they wait for Guerrilla's missions and scripts, which come last of all.
- There's no music or radio yet, and some of Guerrilla's materials and shaders are still Armageddon's.
- If the game freezes, wait a few seconds before closing it: the log then records where it was stuck. Please send `logs\rfa_mods.log`.

## The Guerrilla world

**Coming from any earlier version:** your world has to be built again (the launcher says so, and opens the builder if you choose No). Run `editor\GuerrillaBuilder.exe` and click **Build**: everything is ticked.

## Trying the Guerrilla world for the first time

1. You need **Red Faction: Guerrilla Steam Edition** installed (the original, `Red Faction Guerrilla` in your Steam library; Re-MARS-tered's files aren't read yet), and about 3 GB free where RFA-Mod is.
2. Run `editor\GuerrillaBuilder.exe`. If it doesn't find Guerrilla by itself, click **Browse...** beside Red Faction: Guerrilla and choose its folder.
3. Leave everything ticked and click **Build** (a few minutes).
4. Start the game with `RFAModLauncher.exe`, open the developer console with the ` key (from the main menu is fine) and type `level rfg_12_11`.
5. To drive:
   1. Press **F7**, click **Spawn**, then **Vehicles**, pick one and click Spawn.
   2. Press F7 again and get in with E.
6. For a gun: **F7 → Spawn → Weapons**, type `rfg_` and click Give. Press **Tab** for the world map, and Esc for the pause menu: the Handbook and SAVE GAME are there.

To take it all away again, tick **World** in the builder and click **Remove...**.

## You need

- The Steam version of the game on the **publicbeta** branch: in Steam, right-click the game, then Properties → Game Versions & Betas → **publicbeta**. On other builds the game runs normally and the mods leave it alone.
- Windows 10 or 11.
- For the editor and the builder: the 64-bit (x64) [.NET 10 Desktop Runtime](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/10.0).

## Installing

Unzip into a folder of your own and set `GameDir=` in `rfa_mods.ini` to your game folder (or start the editor or the builder once and pick the folder there). Or unzip it straight into the game folder. Then run `RFAModLauncher.exe` with Steam running. The README has the details.

**Updating from v0.6.6-beta:** copy the new files over your old ones, but keep your own `rfa_mods.ini` and `console.ini`. The launcher adds any new settings to your file and keeps your values. The nine old `mods\guerrilla_*.dll` files are no longer used and can be deleted, but keep the `mods\guerrilla_*` folders: they hold your built world's data. Then build the world again, as above.

## Please note

- This is a beta, and the Guerrilla world a work in progress: expect rough edges, and please report problems as an issue here. `logs\rfa_mods.log`, `logs\guerrilla_builder.log`, a crash's `logs\crash-*.dmp` and `version.txt` help.
- For single-player only. Don't use the mods in online multiplayer.
