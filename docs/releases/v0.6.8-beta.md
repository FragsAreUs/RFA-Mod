A beta of RFA-Mod: mods and an editor for the Steam version of Red Faction: Armageddon. Nothing in your game folder is ever changed; the mods work in the game's memory after it starts.

**Download:** `RFA-Mod-v0.6.8-beta.zip` below.

## What's new since v0.6.7-beta

**Heavy Metal, Guerrilla's area defense.**
- Walk up to one of its nine sites on foot and press E to start. Each site gives you a walker, a tank or a turret.
- Hold the ring while the EDF attacks in waves of vehicles or soldiers. The attacks grow as you destroy more of them, or as time passes.
- The corner panel counts your kills against the number needed. Leave the ring for 10 seconds and you fail.
- Win, and the EDF's control of the district drops and you earn salvage. Guerrilla's COMPLETE stamp shows.
- Lose (out of the ring, or dead), and Guerrilla's results screen offers **RETRY**, which starts the site again, or **EXIT**.

**The sky.**
- The clouds cast shadows that drift over the ground, as in Re-Mars-tered. They're on by default; `CloudShadows=0` turns them off (they need `SunShadows=1` and `Clouds=1`).
- Fixed: the sky, clouds and haze flickering while you move, especially when driving across zones.

**Speed and the log.**
- The mods' own work on the game's main thread is about seven times lighter: about 0.24 ms a frame, down from 1.8 ms.
- `logs\rfa_mods.log` now holds only problems (errors, warnings, crashes and the game's own reports), plus one line from each part as it starts. Each part's `Log=` adds its detail lines when you're chasing a fault; off, they cost nothing.
- The console shows only those problems.
- For mod makers: `[Loader] Profile=1` logs how much of each frame each mod's code takes. When you build from the source, `build.cmd` writes a `.map` file beside each DLL, which names the addresses it lists.

**Also:**
- EDF squads' cars are no longer held to Armageddon's limit of 16 at once: the Guerrilla world's pool of 64 is used.
- Fewer "Failed pathfind" errors indoors: people no longer try paths the game can't find.

Everything from v0.6.7-beta is here too: Transporter, killing spree, mayhem, the car bomb, and sounds that fade with distance.

**Known problems:**
- **The haze on the ground ends in a hard line** at mid distance. The cloud shadows make it easier to see.
- **The walker given at a Heavy Metal site** can fall under the map.
- **Some Heavy Metal sites have scripted extras** in Guerrilla. They come with Guerrilla's scripts, later.
- **Picking up a propane tank for the first time** can put it in your weapon select, with a large shape stuck to Mason's hands. Change weapons, then pick the tank up again.
- **Cars stop dead at walls**, and at many signs and poles, instead of breaking through.
- **Steering may still be rough at speed.**
- **Guerrilla's walkers walk on the spot** when you drive one.
- **Mason can't take cover** against walls yet.
- The activities, the EDF's reinforcements and the vehicle drop-off are new, and haven't had much testing in game yet.
- **Mission briefing terminals** do nothing yet: they wait for Guerrilla's missions and scripts, which come last of all.
- There's no music or radio yet, and some of Guerrilla's materials and shaders are still Armageddon's.
- If the game freezes, wait a few seconds before closing it: the log then records where it was stuck. Please send `logs\rfa_mods.log`.

## The Guerrilla world

**Coming from any earlier version:** your world has to be built again (the launcher says so, and opens the builder if you choose No). Run `editor\GuerrillaBuilder.exe` and click **Build**: everything is ticked.

## Trying the Guerrilla world for the first time

1. You need **Red Faction: Guerrilla Steam Edition** installed (the original, `Red Faction Guerrilla` in your Steam library; Re-MARS-tered's files aren't read yet), and about 3 GB free where RFA-Mod is.
2. Run `editor\GuerrillaBuilder.exe`. If it doesn't find Guerrilla by itself, click **Browse...** beside Red Faction: Guerrilla and choose its folder.
3. Leave everything ticked and click **Build** (a few minutes).
4. Start the game with `RFAModLauncher.exe`, open the developer console with the ` key (from the main menu is fine) and type `level rfg_12_11`.
5. To drive:
   1. Press **F7**, click **Spawn**, then **Vehicles**, pick one and click Spawn.
   2. Press F7 again and get in with E.
6. For a gun: **F7 → Spawn → Weapons**, type `rfg_` and click Give. Press **Tab** for the world map, and Esc for the pause menu: the Handbook and SAVE GAME are there.

To take it all away again, tick **World** in the builder and click **Remove...**.

## You need

- The Steam version of the game on the **publicbeta** branch: in Steam, right-click the game, then Properties → Game Versions & Betas → **publicbeta**. On other builds the game runs normally and the mods leave it alone.
- Windows 10 or 11.
- For the editor and the builder: the 64-bit (x64) [.NET 10 Desktop Runtime](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/10.0).

## Installing

Unzip into a folder of your own and set `GameDir=` in `rfa_mods.ini` to your game folder (or start the editor or the builder once and pick the folder there). Or unzip it straight into the game folder. Then run `RFAModLauncher.exe` with Steam running. The README has the details.

**Updating from v0.6.7-beta:** copy the new files over your old ones, but keep your own `rfa_mods.ini` and `console.ini`. The launcher adds any new settings to your file and keeps your values. Then build the world again, as above.

## Please note

- This is a beta, and the Guerrilla world a work in progress: expect rough edges, and please report problems as an issue here. `logs\rfa_mods.log`, `logs\guerrilla_builder.log`, a crash's `logs\crash-*.dmp` and `version.txt` help.
- For single-player only. Don't use the mods in online multiplayer.
