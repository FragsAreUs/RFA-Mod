A beta of RFA-Mod: mods and an editor for the Steam version of Red Faction: Armageddon. Nothing in your game folder is ever changed; the mods work in the game's memory after it starts.

**Download:** `RFA-Mod-v0.6.9-beta.zip` below.

## What's new since v0.6.8-beta

An update for the level editor and the Guerrilla builder only. The mods and the Guerrilla world are the same as in v0.6.8-beta.

**Listen to the Guerrilla world's sounds in the editor.** RFA-Mod now has its own sound engine, which plays the sound banks the Guerrilla builder makes through OpenAL Soft. Pick a bank (a `.bnk_pc` under **Explorer → build\pc\cache → New files**) and:
- its events are listed, with a search box;
- **Play** and **Stop** post an event as the game would: a loop plays until stopped, a gun's burst ends in its release; **Stop all** stops everything;
- drag the sound around you on the pad to hear its distance and which side it's on (the pad's edge can be 10 m to 400 m away);
- set its game parameters (an engine's RPM, ...) and switches, and see each voice playing;
- **Guerrilla's original** plays the sound the event was made from, out of your own copy of Red Faction: Guerrilla, so you can compare.

Armageddon's own banks list and describe their events, but don't play yet.

**The Guerrilla builder checks the sounds.** Its check now also plays every event of the world's 40 sound banks silently, and reports any that don't end as they should, stutter, or are refused by their voice limits.

This is the first step towards playing Guerrilla's sounds in the game through the same engine, so that Armageddon's sound limits no longer cut them off. That part comes later.

`docs\editor-guide.md` has the details.

## Updating

**From v0.6.8-beta:** copy the new files over your old ones, but keep your own `rfa_mods.ini` and `console.ini`. Your Guerrilla world doesn't need building again.

**From anything older:** follow the v0.6.8-beta notes: after copying the files, build the Guerrilla world again (`editor\GuerrillaBuilder.exe`, then **Build**; the launcher tells you).

## You need

- The Steam version of the game on the **publicbeta** branch: in Steam, right-click the game, then Properties → Game Versions & Betas → **publicbeta**.
- Windows 10 or 11.
- For the editor and the builder: the 64-bit (x64) [.NET 10 Desktop Runtime](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/10.0).

## Please note

- This is a beta: please report problems as an issue here. `logs\rfa_mods.log`, the editor's `logs\editor.log`, `logs\guerrilla_builder.log` and `version.txt` help.
- For single-player only. Don't use the mods in online multiplayer.
