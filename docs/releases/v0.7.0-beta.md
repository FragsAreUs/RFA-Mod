A beta of RFA-Mod: mods and an editor for the Steam version of Red Faction: Armageddon. Nothing in your game folder is ever changed; the mods work in the game's memory after it starts.

**Download:** `RFA-Mod-v0.7.0-beta.zip` below.

## What's new since v0.6.9-beta

**Convoys and the GPS.**
- Guerrilla's convoys roll through the world. A corner panel counts the vehicles left and how far the target is.
- In a "capture the target vehicle" convoy, damage the target (or stop it) and its crew jumps out, as in Guerrilla. Then take it to a safehouse. Destroying the target fails the activity, as it does in Guerrilla.
- Press **G** to set the GPS beacon on the activity on offer. An orange route from you to it shows on the world map and the minimap, and it updates as you drive.

**The world map.**
- Guerrilla's fog of war: the map is under drifting clouds until you've been there. What you've uncovered is kept in your saves.
- Fixed: the map going black when zoomed out, and a second player arrow when you hover over yourself.
- In a vehicle the crosshair is hidden; a mounted gun's overheat bar still shows while you fire it.

**People and traffic.**
- Traffic appears further out and out of sight (Guerrilla's own rules), not right in front of you.
- Traffic drivers are civilians: EDF patrols leave them alone, unless they join your fight.
- Guerrilla's friendly fire: kill a civilian or a Red Faction fighter at green alert, or keep shooting a fighter, and the Red Faction fighters nearby turn on you. They calm down 20 seconds later.

**Guns.**
- Fixed: the game freezing when the nano rifle dissolves a vehicle with people in it, or a vehicle that loses parts.
- The Enforcer draws no tracer, as in Guerrilla's single player; the other guns get their tracers.

**From Delgeth's work** (merged in this release):
- **RFA Animation Tools 1.6**, a Blender add-on (`tools\blender` in the source): characters, animations, vehicle building, vehicle animations (getting in and out, doors), a vehicle parameter editor with templates and a sound browser.
- The level editor: a script editor for logic handlers, tables that update live in the running game, and a cache of what it read from the game's archives, so it starts much faster after the first time.
- A faster game start, Guerrilla's controller layout in vehicles, and Armageddon's flyer controls for Guerrilla's flyers (an F7 option).
- Enemies fight in Ruin levels (Options > Experimentals).

**Settings.**
- A settings window: run `editor\RfaSettings.exe`, or click **Mod settings** in the editor. Every setting of `rfa_mods.ini` is there with its explanation, a search box and reset to default, and it says which settings take effect only when the game starts. Saving changes only what you changed.

**Sound (new, and off unless you turn it on).**
- RFA-Mod's own sound engine, on OpenAL Soft, can play the game's sounds in place of Wwise: set `[Audio] OwnPlayer=1` in `rfa_mods.ini`. `Banks=` chooses which sound banks it plays (empty: the Guerrilla world's; `rfg*, wep_*, interface*, sfx_player` adds Armageddon's guns and menus). It isn't held to the game's sound limits, and `HRTF=1` gives 3D sound on headphones.
- The editor's sound preview plays Armageddon's own sound banks as well as Guerrilla's, and shows their real names.

The Guerrilla world's activities, killing spree, mayhem and the rest from v0.6.8-beta are all here too.

**Known problems:**
- **People in the Guerrilla world may say nothing**: their voice lines aren't being started at all in recent tests (the kiosks' broadcasts play). This is being looked into.
- **The new sound engine (`OwnPlayer=1`) is still being tested.** If anything sounds wrong, set `OwnPlayer=0` (the default) to go back to the game's own.
- **Windows Defender may remove `RFAModLauncher.exe`** as "Behavior:Win32/DefenseEvasion.A!ml". It's a guess from how the launcher starts the game with the mods; the file is the one built from this release's source. To keep it: Windows Security → Virus & threat protection → Protection history → the entry → Actions → **Allow on device**.
- **The GPS route** shows on the world map and the minimap; the route drawn on the ground comes later.
- **The haze on the ground ends in a hard line** at mid distance.
- **Cars stop dead at walls**, and at many signs and poles, instead of breaking through.
- **Guerrilla's walkers walk on the spot** when you drive one, and **Mason can't take cover** yet.
- The activities, the EDF's reinforcements and the vehicle drop-off are still new and haven't had much testing.
- **Mission briefing terminals** do nothing yet: they wait for Guerrilla's missions and scripts, which come last of all.
- There's no music or radio yet.
- If the game freezes, wait a few seconds before closing it: the log then records where it was stuck. Please send `logs\rfa_mods.log`.

## The Guerrilla world

**Coming from any earlier version:** your world has to be built again (each road is now written once, which the GPS and the drivers need). The launcher says so, and opens the builder if you choose No. Run `editor\GuerrillaBuilder.exe` and click **Build**: everything is ticked.

## Trying the Guerrilla world for the first time

1. You need **Red Faction: Guerrilla Steam Edition** installed (the original, `Red Faction Guerrilla` in your Steam library; Re-MARS-tered's files aren't read yet), and about 3 GB free where RFA-Mod is.
2. Run `editor\GuerrillaBuilder.exe`. If it doesn't find Guerrilla by itself, click **Browse...** beside Red Faction: Guerrilla and choose its folder.
3. Leave everything ticked and click **Build** (a few minutes).
4. Start the game with `RFAModLauncher.exe`, open the developer console with the ` key (from the main menu is fine) and type `level rfg_12_11`.
5. To drive:
   1. Press **F7**, click **Spawn**, then **Vehicles**, pick one and click Spawn.
   2. Press F7 again and get in with E.
6. For a gun: **F7 → Spawn → Weapons**, type `rfg_` and click Give. Press **Tab** for the world map, and Esc for the pause menu: the Handbook and SAVE GAME are there.

To take it all away again, tick **World** in the builder and click **Remove...**.

## You need

- The Steam version of the game on the **publicbeta** branch: in Steam, right-click the game, then Properties → Game Versions & Betas → **publicbeta**. On other builds the game runs normally and the mods leave it alone.
- Windows 10 or 11.
- For the editor, the builder and the settings window: the 64-bit (x64) [.NET 10 Desktop Runtime](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/10.0).

## Installing

Unzip into a folder of your own and set `GameDir=` in `rfa_mods.ini` to your game folder (or start the editor or the builder once and pick the folder there). Or unzip it straight into the game folder. Then run `RFAModLauncher.exe` with Steam running. The README has the details.

**Updating from an earlier version:** copy the new files over your old ones, but keep your own `rfa_mods.ini` and `console.ini`. The launcher adds any new settings to your file and keeps your values. Then build the world again, as above.

## Please note

- This is a beta, and the Guerrilla world a work in progress: expect rough edges, and please report problems as an issue here. `logs\rfa_mods.log`, `logs\guerrilla_builder.log`, a crash's `logs\crash-*.dmp` and `version.txt` help.
- For single-player only. Don't use the mods in online multiplayer.
